Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 22 November 2026

Welcome to ToolBox Hub. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our website handles information when you use our online tools and services.

1. Information We Collect

ToolBox Hub is designed to provide browser-based tools. Our current tools do not require users to create accounts or provide personal information to use their main features.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through our online calculators, word counter or image compressor.

2. How Our Tools Handle Your Data

  • Word Counter: Text is processed in your browser.
  • Calculators: Values are calculated in your browser.
  • QR Code Generator: QR codes are generated using a third-party JavaScript library loaded from a CDN.
  • Image Compressor: Images are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to our server.
  • Unit Converter: Conversion calculations happen in your browser.

3. Third-Party Services

Our QR Code Generator currently loads a JavaScript library from Cloudflare's cdnjs content delivery network. When your browser requests this library, the service may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services. Please review their respective privacy policies.

4. Cookies

Our current website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or user tracking. Third-party services may have their own data practices.

5. Data Security

We aim to keep our website simple and minimize the information handled by our tools. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website provides general-purpose tools. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into any online tool.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or services change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact details provided on our Contact page once available.
